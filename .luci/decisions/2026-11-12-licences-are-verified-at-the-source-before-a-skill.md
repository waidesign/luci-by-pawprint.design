# Licences are verified at the source before a skill ships

- Date: 2026-11-12
- Status: accepted
- Area: Skills

## What
Licences are verified at the source before a skill ships

## Why
Only skills whose licence allows redistribution ship (S6). The prototype's import scripts wrote license: MIT into skills they fetched from the ui-skills registry, so those labels are not evidence. Each of the 17 unverified design skills is traced to its original public repository and its licence read there; open ones ship with the evidence recorded in luci.json, the rest stay out. The user confirmed accessibility-baseline is their own and ships under MIT.
