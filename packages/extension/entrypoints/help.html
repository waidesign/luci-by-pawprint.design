Capturing design with Luci

Luci turns what you pick on a live page into a design.md file your AI coding agent can use. Everything stays on your machine.

Three steps

  1. Pick a tool and click. Hover the page: the label shows the element's tag and size. Click what you want.
  2. Adjust the selection. The element stays selected. ↑ Wider selects the element around it, ↓ Narrower the one inside it. The ↑ ↓ keys do the same.
  3. Choose an output. This one captures the item; The whole system captures everything like it on the page. Then copy the file, download it, or add it to the basket.

The tools

  • Component: a button, card, link or field: its parts, tokens, motion and states (hover, focus, active, disabled). The whole system adds its variants on the page.
  • Text: a text style. The whole system lists every text style on the page, grouped into roles.
  • Color: click an element for its color, or switch to Pixel and hover the page to see the color under the pointer, then click. Luci's own panels are never picked.
  • Spacing: hover to see padding and margin, click for a value, or drag from one element to another to measure the distance between them.

States done in JavaScript

Luci reads hover, focus and active states exactly. While it does, Chrome shows a "debugging this browser" bar for a moment. If a state only changes through JavaScript, it shows Not detected: choose it under State, press Capture current state, then hover, focus or press the component during the 3-second countdown.

The basket

Press Add to basket after any capture. Basket in the bar keeps your picks across pages and sites until you empty it. Rename items, remove them, export one, or Export all as one file.

Keys

  • ↑ ↓ widen or narrow the selection, while hovering or after clicking
  • Enter picks, and after clicking takes This one
  • Esc cancels, or closes the panel

Good to know

  • Images, video and canvas can't be captured; they are greyed out with the reason.
  • Chrome's own pages and the Web Store can't be captured by any extension.
  • After you update or reload Luci, reload the page and click Luci's icon again.
