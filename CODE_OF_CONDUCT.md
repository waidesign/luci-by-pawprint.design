# Code of Conduct

Luci follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, welcome people of every background and experience level, give and
take feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults, personal
or political attacks, sexualised language or imagery, and publishing others' private information are
not acceptable, in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere you represent the
project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, email **lucas@pawprint.design**. Reports are
handled privately and promptly. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions, and
temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code, following the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
